Refund & Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Who we are

CardPrime is operated by CardPrime Korlátolt Felelősségű Társaság ("CardPrime", "we", "us"). This policy applies to purchases of sealed card packs made through the CardPrime app webshop.

Contact: info@cardprime.io

2. Orders and payment

Orders are placed and paid for in-app via our payment processor, Stripe. An order is confirmed once payment has been successfully collected. You'll receive a payment receipt from Stripe and, where applicable, a VAT invoice by email.

3. Shipping

Sealed packs are physical goods and are shipped to the address or parcel locker you provide at checkout. Shipping times and carriers will be communicated at checkout and by email once your order ships. For consumer purchases, risk of loss or damage in transit stays with CardPrime until you (or a recipient you designate, other than the carrier itself) actually receive the parcel — not upon handover to the carrier.

4. Right to cancel — 14-day withdrawal (statutory)

If you're a consumer (a private individual, not buying for your trade, business, or profession) in Hungary or elsewhere in the EU, you have a statutory right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving your order, for any reason, without needing to justify it. This is separate from, and broader than, the pre-shipment cancellation in Section 5 and the "no refund for randomness" clause in Section 6.

To exercise this right, contact info@cardprime.io within the 14-day period with a clear statement that you're withdrawing (a model withdrawal form is available on request). You must send the goods back within 14 days of that notice, at your own shipping cost, and we'll refund you — including standard delivery cost, if any — within 14 days of receiving the returned goods or proof that you've sent them, whichever comes first.

5. Cancelling before shipment

Separately from the statutory withdrawal right above — and simpler, since it needs no form — you may cancel an order free of charge at any time before it has shipped (i.e. while its status is "pending payment" or "paid" but not yet dispatched). To cancel, email info@cardprime.io with your order number. Once an order has shipped, use the withdrawal right in Section 4 or the returns process in Section 10 instead.

6. Sealed-pack contents and your withdrawal right

Each pack's contents are randomized by design — that unpredictability is the product. Being unhappy with which card(s) you received isn't, by itself, grounds for a discretionary refund or exchange beyond what's described in this policy, whether the outcome was better or worse than you expected.

This does not limit your statutory withdrawal right under Section 4. If you withdraw after opening a pack, we may deduct a value-diminishment amount, calculated as: the pack's purchase price minus the fair market value (at the time of your return) of the card(s) you actually received. This reflects that an opened pack can no longer be resold as new, while the card(s) you pulled keep their own resale value — so the deduction is limited to what you've genuinely lost the ability to resell, not the full price.

7. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your pack arrives damaged, defective, or if we shipped you the wrong item, contact info@cardprime.io within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the issue. We will offer a replacement or a full refund.

8. Non-delivery or lost packages

Most orders are expected to arrive within a few business days of shipping; the specific estimate for your order is communicated at checkout and by email once it ships. If your order hasn't arrived within that window plus a reasonable buffer, contact info@cardprime.io with your order number. We'll check the shipment status with our carrier; if the parcel is confirmed lost, or can't be located within a reasonable investigation period, we'll send a replacement or issue a full refund, your choice.

9. Refund method and timing

Refunds under this policy (Sections 4, 7, or 8) are issued to your original payment method via Stripe. Refunds are typically initiated within 5 business days of approval; the time for the funds to appear depends on your card issuer or bank.

10. Returns

Because pack contents are sealed and randomized, we do not accept discretionary returns of opened packs except under Section 7 (damaged/defective/incorrect) or your statutory withdrawal right under Section 4. Unopened, undamaged packs may also be returned within 14 days of delivery at your own shipping cost — contact info@cardprime.io first to arrange this.
