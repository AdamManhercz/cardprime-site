1.Who runs the prize game

The CardPrime prize game is organised by CardPrime Korlátolt Felelősségű Társaság ("CardPrime", "we", "us"), a company registered in Hungary.

Registered office: 2085 Pilisvörösvár, Semmelweis köz 1., Hungary
Company registration number: 13-09-248460
Email: info@cardprime.io

These rules complement our Terms of Service, which set out the general framework for prize games.

2.Apple is not a sponsor

Apple Inc. is not a sponsor of the CardPrime prize game and is not involved in it in any way. Questions or complaints about the prize game go to CardPrime, not to Apple.

3.Who can take part

Anyone with a valid CardPrime account can take part. A CardPrime account can only be created by someone aged 16 or over.

4.Points

Points are earned in the CardPrime app, mainly by scanning printed CardPrime cards into your collection, by completing weekly quests and by registering for auctions. How many points each activity is worth is shown on the Points page and in the app.

Points only decide your place on the leaderboard, your level and your part in prize games. Points have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

5.How the winners are decided

There is no draw. The winners are the users in the top 15 places of the leaderboard when it closes at the end of the prize game, subject to the eligibility condition in Section 6.

6.Who can receive a prize

A prize can only go to a user who has at least one card in their collection that they scanned themselves and that is still assigned to their account. We check this when the leaderboard closes.

We do not ask for parental consent from winners under 18. We arrange the handover of each prize with the winner directly.

7.Prizes

The prizes of each prize game are the ones set out in its campaign-specific rules (Section 9). A prize shown earlier elsewhere, or as an example, is not a prize unless those rules list it.

8.Suspended or deleted accounts

While an account is suspended, it does not appear on the leaderboard and cannot take part in a prize game. Deleting an account deletes its points for good, including during a prize game that is still running.

9.Campaign-specific rules

Each prize game runs as its own campaign. CardPrime card series can come from different sports and be launched for different occasions, so each campaign has its own rules. Before each prize game starts, we publish its details on this page: its start and end dates, its prizes and any further conditions. They are also available in the CardPrime app under Settings → How Points Work.
