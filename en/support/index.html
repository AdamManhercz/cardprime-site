Frequently asked questions

Is there a prize game, and what can I win?

Yes. A CardPrime prize game is won on the leaderboard, with no draw: the collectors in the top 15 places when the leaderboard closes win, as long as they have at least one card in their collection that they scanned themselves. You collect points by adding cards, completing weekly quests and registering for auctions. The first prize game runs from 1 December 2026 to 5 January 2027, with a training session with the FTC-Telekom Waterpolo senior team, Champions League tickets and more to win: see all the prizes on the Prize Game Rules page. Apple is not a sponsor of the prize game and is not involved in it.

What is CardPrime?

CardPrime prints and sells its own range of sports cards. In the CardPrime app you scan the printed cards you own to build your collection and earn points, buy sealed packs, and bid in auctions for individual cards. The app is available for iPhone; you need a CardPrime account to use it, and you must be 16 or over to create one.

How do I add a card to my collection?

Tap Scan in the middle of the tab bar and point the camera at the code on your CardPrime card. The app first checks that the card is genuine. If no one owns that copy yet, tap Add to my collection and confirm with Face ID or your phone's passcode. A card belongs to one collector at a time, so whoever adds it first keeps it. Cards can only be added by scanning them with the camera: there is no manual entry and no photo upload.

How can I tell whether a card is genuine?

Scan it in the app. Anyone can check any CardPrime card, including one that is already in somebody else's collection: a green Genuine CardPrime card result means the code matches a card we printed. If the scan says Invalid code or failed scan, try again in good light with the card flat and steady inside the frame. If it still fails, or you suspect a counterfeit, email us (see the last question below).

The app says "Already scanned" — can I still get the card?

That copy is already in another collector's collection, so it can't be added to yours. You can tap Request this card to ask the owner for it, or offer a swap of one to five of your own cards. If the owner accepts, you both see the contact details the other one shared, and you arrange the rest between yourselves — CardPrime takes no part in any price or payment you agree on. When you meet, you scan the card, and the owner confirms the handover with Face ID or their passcode; only then does the card move to your collection. Requests expire after 14 days without an answer. A card whose owner deleted their account can't be requested.

What happens if I remove a card from my collection?

You keep the points it earned. Until another collector scans it and adds it to their own collection, you can add it back by scanning it again. Once someone else has added it, it's theirs.

Who can see my collection?

Your username, score and place on the leaderboard are public. Your collection itself is visible to other collectors, who can open it from the leaderboard, unless you hide it: tap the three-dot button at the top of your collection and choose Hide my collection. Hiding it keeps your score and leaderboard place exactly as they are. Others only ever see your username — never your email address. You can change your username at any time in Settings → Edit Profile; a nickname works just as well.

How do points and the leaderboard work?

You earn points by adding cards to your collection (rarer cards are worth more), by completing quests, including the weekly quests, and by registering for auctions. Your points set your level and your place on the leaderboard. Points have no cash value, and they are lost for good if you delete your account. The details are on the Points page and under Settings → How Points Work in the app. Prize games run on the leaderboard: see the Prize Game Rules.

How do auctions work?

Before you can bid you register for that auction with a delivery address and a payment card. The card is stored by our payment provider, Stripe — we never see the numbers — and it is charged only if you win: the winning bid plus shipping, right after the auction ends. Registration is for one auction only, so you register again for the next one. A bid in the final 5 seconds restarts the countdown from 5 seconds, and the highest bid at the close is binding. Auction cards are delivered by GLS (a flat 1,500 Ft within Hungary; abroad, the GLS rate for that country) or can be collected free in person at the King of Hoops card store in Budapest. If the winning charge is declined, we email the winner about paying by bank transfer within 48 hours of the close. The full rules are in the Terms of Service.

How are pack orders delivered, and can I return them?

Sealed packs from the shop can be delivered to a parcel locker or to your address, or collected free in person at the King of Hoops card store in Budapest. Delivery options, fees, the 14-day right of withdrawal and how refunds work are all on the Refund & Shipping page.

How do I report a collection, or block a collector?

Open their collection (for example from the leaderboard), tap the three-dot button at the top and choose Report this collection or Block this collector. Our team reviews every report without undue delay, normally within 24–48 hours, and a collection reported by several people is hidden automatically in the meantime. Blocking works both ways: their collection disappears for you and yours for them, and you drop off each other's leaderboard. You can see your reports, read our decisions and undo any block under Settings → Blocked Accounts & Reports. In a card request, you can also report the contact details the other collector shared. If you'd rather write to a person, email info@cardprime.io — we respond to every report we receive.

What isn't allowed?

Offensive, hateful or impersonating usernames, harassment, spam, and abusive or misleading contact details in card requests — or anything else that is sexual, violent, hateful, illegal or deliberately deceptive. Accounts that break these rules or abuse other collectors may be suspended or terminated. See the Terms of Service for the full rules.

Is my location tracked?

No continuous or background tracking. When you scan a card, the app can record your approximate location, rounded to about 1.1 km, used only to spot scan patterns that suggest a copied code. It's optional: scanning works the same without location permission.

Where is my data stored?

Your account and collection data are stored on AWS infrastructure in the EU (the Stockholm region). Card payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see your card numbers. The full list of what we store, why, and who processes it is in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your collection and your points, and it can't be undone. The printed cards you scanned can't be added by anyone else afterwards. Order and invoice records are kept for eight years, as the law requires, and scan and bid history is kept without your name. If you signed in with Apple, the app also asks you to confirm in the Apple window, which disconnects CardPrime from your Apple ID.

I found a bug or suspect a counterfeit card — who do I contact?

Email info@cardprime.io with as much detail as possible: the card's player and design, screenshots, and what you expected to happen.
