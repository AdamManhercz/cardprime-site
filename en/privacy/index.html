3.What others can see

  • Leaderboard: your username, points and rank.
  • Public collection (the default): your username and the Cards in your collection. You can make it private in Settings at any time; then nobody sees what is in your collection, and on any of your Cards "Private collector" is shown instead of your username.
  • Card requests: the owner of the Card you request sees your username; your contact details become visible to them only after acceptance.
  • Auctions: only your alias and bid amount; we never publish the winner's details.

Other users never see your email address, name, location or scan log. Nobody can search for you by username.

Authorised CardPrime staff can see a Card's history in the admin interface — the scanner's username, the time, result and approximate location of each scan — but only to detect counterfeits and abuse, investigate complaints and moderate.

4.Legal basis

Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — your account, sign-in, scanning and collection, points and leaderboard, card requests, fulfilling and shipping orders, auction registration and settlement, delivering prizes.

Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — keeping invoices and accounting records (Hungarian Act C of 2000 on Accounting, section 169), VAT and OSS returns, handling consumer complaints, and the notice mechanism for illegal content under the EU Digital Services Act.

Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — detecting counterfeits and abuse (scan log, approximate location, card history), moderation (reports, blocks, username filter, violation count), security logs, aggregate analysis of the weekly surveys, and keeping pseudonymised history after account deletion (see section 7). These protect the authenticity of Cards, the fairness of the points competition and other users' safety, and are limited to what is needed. On the website, loading Google Tag Manager and the cookieless Google Analytics pings serve our interest in serving the site and estimating visits.

Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — location access and push notifications (via your phone's permission prompt; withdraw any time in your phone's settings), and on the website the cookie-based Google Analytics, Google signals and Meta Pixel (withdraw via the "Cookie settings" link). The cookie that stores your choice is strictly necessary for the cookie banner to work.

What happens when you delete your account. Deletion in the app is automatic, not a manual or delayed process:

  • we delete your profile, sign-in data (Cognito), sign-in log, notifications, reports and blocks in both directions, auction registrations, likes, weekly answers, card requests and your username; for Apple sign-in we revoke Apple access, and we delete the payment method saved with Stripe;
  • your points are lost for good (Terms chapter 14);
  • the Cards in your collection remain taken: nobody else can add a deleted account's Card to their collection;
  • in card histories and the scan log your account is replaced by a pseudonymous identifier that does not identify you; closed auctions keep your alias;
  • orders and invoices are kept for 8 years because of the accounting obligation.

Backups. Our database has point-in-time recovery enabled, which continuously keeps a rolling 35-day window so we can restore the system after an outage, a faulty deployment or accidental data loss. It is a whole-database safety net, not a per-user archive: individual records cannot be removed from it, so for up to 35 days after deletion your data may still exist in this recovery window. The app cannot access it, we never use it to serve or restore individual accounts, and it expires automatically.

8.Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you;
  • rectify inaccurate data (you can change your name and username in the app);
  • erasure — except data we must keep by law (invoices);
  • restrict processing in certain cases;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests (section 4);
  • withdraw consent at any time: location and notifications in your phone's settings, website cookies via the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing;
  • data portability — receiving the data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11., Hungary, naih.hu; or with the authority of your own EU/EEA country of residence. You may also go to court.

Deleting your account: in the app under Settings → Delete account. Everything described in section 7 happens immediately and automatically. It cannot be undone.

For any other request, or if you cannot access the app, email info@cardprime.io. We reply within one month at the latest.

9.Automated processing

We do not profile you and do not use artificial intelligence on your personal data. The following automatic steps are part of how the service works:

  • Card verification. When you scan, the system checks that the Card's QR code is genuine.
  • First scanner wins. A Card not yet assigned to anyone goes to the first person who scans it successfully (Terms chapter 3).
  • Auctions. The highest valid bid wins, and after closing the system automatically charges the winner's saved payment method (Terms chapter 9). Stripe runs its own fraud checks on payments.
  • Abuse flag. If a Card's scans show a physically implausible place and time, the system sets an internal flag. That alone triggers no action.
  • Report threshold and username filter. If several different users report the same content, it is hidden until a human reviews it; the system does not accept offensive usernames.

Only a person can decide to suspend or delete an account. You can contest any outcome at info@cardprime.io.

10.Children's data

CardPrime is for collectors aged 16 and over — in Hungary this is also the age from which a person can consent on their own to an online service processing their personal data.

How we apply the requirement. Being at least 16 is a condition of the Terms (section 2.10), which you accept when you register. By doing so and by continuing to use CardPrime you declare that you meet it. We deliberately do not ask for your date of birth and do not verify age with identity documents: collecting documents from every user would gather far more personal data than it could ever protect. Here the declaration is the proportionate measure, and we call it exactly what it is: a declaration, not verification.

Because we do not verify, we cannot rule out that someone under 16 uses CardPrime despite the requirement. What we do:

  • we never knowingly collect data from anyone under 16;
  • if we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we delete it as described in section 7;
  • anyone — including a parent or guardian — can report such an account to info@cardprime.io and exercise the rights in section 8 on the child's behalf.

If you are between 16 and 18, please make sure your parent or guardian knows you use the app, especially if you buy something or bid in an auction.

11.How we protect your data

  • All communication between the app, the website and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • The database and storage are encrypted at rest; we never see your password, and only Stripe handles your card details.
  • Completing a card request requires Face ID or your device passcode.
  • Every server-side component runs on the principle of least privilege, and only authorised staff can access the admin interface.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we report it to the NAIH within 72 hours and inform you as required by GDPR Articles 33–34.
