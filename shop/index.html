Delivery

Orders are dispatched within 2 business days of payment, and standard delivery takes a further 2–5 business days. Delivery costs a flat 1 950 Ft per order, however many packs you buy. We ship to addresses and parcel lockers; the charge and the estimate are shown again at checkout before you pay.

Payment

Payment is handled by Stripe on a secure page hosted by Stripe — we never see or store your card details. You will receive a payment receipt from Stripe and a VAT invoice by email.

Returns and refunds

As a consumer in the EU you may withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of delivery, and damaged, defective, incorrect or undelivered orders are replaced or refunded in full. The full terms, including how a randomized pack is treated on return, are in our Refund & Shipping Policy.
