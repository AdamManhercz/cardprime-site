How points work

Scan printed CardPrime cards, complete quests, and climb from Rookie to Immortal.

The basics

CardPrime has one currency: points. You earn them by scanning printed CardPrime cards in the app, and by completing quests.

  • Every physical card counts. Each printed card gives its own points — pull the same Base card twice and both copies score. Each physical card can only be claimed once, by the first collector who scans it.
  • Rarer cards are worth more. The fewer copies of a card that exist, the more points it gives, and cards of higher-tier players are worth more than the same card of a lower tier. Base cards are 10 points for everyone.
  • Quests give one-time bonuses. Each quest can be completed once and pays a bonus of up to 2,000 points. Quest points are yours to keep, even if the cards later leave your collection.
  • Your score sets your level. Your total points decide your level, and every level comes with its own badge.

Your own score, level and quest progress are shown in the CardPrime app. This page explains the rules — it doesn't show personal progress.

Levels & badges

Seven levels, each with its own badge. Your level always follows your current score — if your score goes down because a card leaves your collection, your level and badge can go down with it.

  1. Level 1Rookie0 pts
  2. Level 2Collector500 pts
  3. Level 3Pro Collector1,500 pts
  4. Level 4Elite3,500 pts
  5. Level 5Master7,000 pts
  6. Level 6Legend12,000 pts
  7. Level 7Immortal20,000 pts

Quests

Every quest can be completed once and pays a one-time bonus. Collection goals count different cards, not copies — two identical Base cards are still one player. “Player” here also includes the head coach, who has his own cards in the set.
